## Recommended fixes (in order of effort)

1. **Add a Playwright MCP server** to `.claude/settings.json` — runs a real headless Chrome that defeats most Cloudflare/Akamai checks. This is the single most impactful change. Roughly 30 min of setup.
2. **Add a third-party scraping MCP** like ScrapingBee, ScraperAPI, or Bright Data — pay-per-request (~$0.01–0.05 each) but extremely reliable. ~10 min setup if you already have an account.
3. **Manually triage**: open Carvana / CarGurus in your browser, copy the 5–10 Armadas that look viable into a single prompt, and Claude does the filter + Carfax fetch + ranking pass on that human-curated input. Zero setup, but a few minutes of your time daily.
4. **Switch to email digests from the sites themselves** — every aggregator (Cars.com, CarGurus, Autotrader, Carvana) can email you a saved-search alert. Not as smart as Claude, but free and unblockable.
